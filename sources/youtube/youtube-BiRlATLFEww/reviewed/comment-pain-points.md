**Source note:** this session had no access to the video's actual YouTube

## Recurring points of confusion this video calls out directly

- **Trying to sell the end product in the ad itself** — named directly as
  "one really common mistake people make": the ad's only job is to get the
  right person to click, not to close the sale; that belongs to the landing
  page and sales process. (00:16:48)
- **One-step vs. two-step funnel confusion** — presenters note that
  business owners often run both without understanding which one they're
  actually optimizing for, and the recommendation is to pick one. (00:19:30)
- **Misreading early results as a verdict** — the "Facebook's gone really
  well" / "Facebook's not working" reflex after only a few days is called
  out as a common business-owner mistake tied to not understanding the
  learning phase and small sample sizes. (00:19:30)
- **What "pixel conditioning" is and why a lead isn't just a lead** — the
  video spends significant time on the idea that beginners generally don't
  realize a wrong-industry or disqualified form submission can quietly
  poison the pixel's targeting signal. (03:07:09–04:36:23)
- **FAQ placement and depth on landing pages** — repeatedly flagged as an
  area operators under-invest in (too few FAQs, placed too low on the page),
  based on the presenters' own split-testing anecdotes. (00:58:33,
  02:57:22–02:58:03, 03:52:14–03:53:15)
- **Whether the buyer needs to personally take the sales call** — flagged as
  a common concern from beginners worried about being the face of every
  call; addressed by "inoculating" (setting expectations) rather than
  requiring the owner to be present. (00:59:53–01:00:34)
