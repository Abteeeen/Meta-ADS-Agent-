## Recurring points of confusion this video calls out directly

- **"Facebook ads" vs. "Instagram ads" vs. "Meta ads"** — the video opens by
  correcting the common assumption that these are separate products; it
  frames all placements (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network) as
  one ecosystem. (00:01:41–00:02:41, 00:07:44–00:08:44)
- **Organic reach vs. paid reach** — "a lot of people confuse this" when
  moving from organic social posting to paid Meta Ads, particularly around
  why organic reach can't be controlled or scaled the way paid can.
  (00:03:02–00:04:23)
- **What the pixel/dataset actually measures, and why it matters** — the
  video repeatedly stresses that without a pixel, neither the advertiser nor
  Meta can tell which ad or audience produced a sale, and frames this as the
  single most common blind spot for beginners setting up their first account.
  (00:49:03–00:55:06)
- **CPC on Meta vs. CPC on Google Ads** — the presenter explicitly flags "don't
  get confused" between the two platforms' differing click-cost metrics and
  what "cost per result" actually means on Meta. (03:01:42)
- **What "the difference" is between a normal delivery and the learning
  phase** — introduced as a question the presenter expects viewers to ask
  ("what me is the difference you might ask"), then walked through in a
  dedicated section. (01:58:44, expanded 06:02:39–06:12:23)
- **CBO vs. ABO terminology** — presented as a point that trips up newcomers
  moving from the old naming to current Ads Manager labels. (01:46:37)
