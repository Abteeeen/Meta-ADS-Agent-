## Recurring points of confusion this video calls out directly

- **What "interest" targeting actually means** — called out directly:
  "a lot of people think this means that you like something, however that's
  completely wrong"; interest groups are built from broader behavioral
  signals (pages/words engaged with), not expressed preference. (00:34:02)
- **What "attribution" means** — called out directly: "a lot of people
  think attribution is around what credit meta takes for results but really
  it's just about what results are you letting meta see." Framed as a
  frequent source of confusion about why an account "under-reports" real
  sales. (00:21:55)
- **How to size a first budget** — the video spends its first block on
  translating "how much should I spend per day" into a runway/total-loss
  calculation, implying this is a question beginners default to asking in
  the wrong unit (daily dollars) before they've defined a total budget and
  timeframe. (00:16:52–00:19:54)
- **Domain/registrar setup friction** — explicitly flagged as varying by
  provider, with the presenter pointing beginners to their domain
  registrar's own FAQ rather than promising one fixed set of steps.
  (00:15:10)
- **Manual placement vs. automatic placement** — framed as a setting
  beginners are tempted to touch ("you will probably never win by choosing
  manual placement... a luxury option for high-end marketers") despite it
  not being needed for most accounts. (00:33:01)
