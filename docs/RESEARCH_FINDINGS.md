## Repeated learner pain points

- Budget allocation: campaign budget versus ad-set budget, and why spend concentrates in one ad.
- Measurement: how to identify a winning ad without mistaking early delivery for performance.
- Setup: pixel/events, domain verification, business manager, payment, and tracking.
- Targeting: lookalikes, interests, broad targeting, and changing Advantage+ controls.
- Operations: who owns creative production and how an agency scopes it with a client.
- Applicability: whether a tactic applies to leads, ecommerce, services, country, or account maturity.

## MVP implications

- The intake must capture business model, offer, market, conversion event, landing destination, budget, attribution context, and account maturity before recommending structure.
- The analyst must show alternate explanations for spend concentration and avoid calling a winner without enough evidence.
- The launch workflow needs setup and tracking checks before optimization advice.
- Creative outputs should be structured as test hypotheses rather than generic copy.
- The tutor mode needs a glossary and contextual explanations for CBO, ABO, pixel, learning phase, CPM, CTR, CPC, CVR, CPA, and ROAS.

These are research leads, not platform facts. Claude should convert each useful finding into a structured claim record with a source location and classification.
