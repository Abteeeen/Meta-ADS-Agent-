# First Vertical: Security Course Agency

The first target vertical is a marketing agency selling a security course. The generic core remains reusable; this document records only the initial operating assumptions to validate with the company.

## Initial operating focus

- Business model: lead generation for course enrollment.
- Primary conversion: qualified enrollment lead, not every form submit.
- Core proof to collect: instructor credibility, curriculum outcomes, student results, course delivery format, and enrollment constraints.
- Primary risks to verify: education claims, job-outcome claims, lead quality, qualification flow, and tracking of qualified versus unqualified inquiries.

## Unknowns to collect before launch

- Course price, payment options, and gross margin.
- Target learner segment and geography.
- Enrollment capacity and sales follow-up process.
- Definition of a qualified lead and feedback path from sales.
- Approved creative assets and claim-review owner.
