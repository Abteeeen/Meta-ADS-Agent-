# Five Star Training Academy: Public Research Brief

**Scope:** Public website and regulator information only. This file deliberately excludes CRM data, lead quality, budgets, seat availability, and other client-confidential operations.

## Verified public context

- Five Star Training Academy is an RTO (No. 31996) offering CPP20218 Certificate II in Security Operations.
- Its public course pages identify Brisbane (Boondall), Gold Coast (Nerang), and Logan (Slacks Creek), alongside regional Queensland locations.
- The course page currently displays a $950 standard course price and references possible Queensland Career Start funding. Price, funding, and eligibility must be checked again before publishing ads.
- The public course-date page embeds a Five Star-branded "2026 Training Calendar." It confirms scheduled-location delivery but does not expose live seat capacity or remaining availability in a reliable, static form.
- The site publicly links to Facebook and Instagram, embeds a YouTube video on location pages, and includes HubSpot collection scripts. These are candidate asset and attribution surfaces, not proof that any particular campaign or conversion event is correctly configured.

## Product guardrails

- Use "train toward a Queensland security licence" or similarly approved wording. Do not imply course completion itself grants a licence.
- Do not promise employment, earnings, funding eligibility, licence approval, or available seats. Those claims need current, client-approved evidence.
- Queensland's regulator states that an unarmed security officer licence also requires age/identity checks, fingerprints, a criminal-history check, and required training. Treat these as pre-enrolment qualification considerations, not ad-targeting attributes.

## Initial operating recommendation

Start with separate Brisbane and Gold Coast acquisition routes so each has its own landing context, course dates, lead-quality measurement, and budget control. Keep Logan and other locations available as an explicit expansion decision rather than mixing all geographies into the first learning cycle.

The primary business outcome is a paid student. The optimization ladder should be:

1. Course enquiry.
2. Eligibility-checked lead.
3. Contacted lead.
4. Enrolment started.
5. Paid student.

## Client inputs required before launch

- Current seats, dates, and enrolment cut-off for each Brisbane and Gold Coast intake.
- Actual monthly test budget and target cost per eligibility-checked lead.
- Approved offer wording, price/funding wording, testimonials, photos, videos, and testimonials-release status.
- HubSpot lifecycle stages and the exact field that marks a lead as eligible, contacted, enrolled, and paid.
- Existing Meta account, Dataset/Pixel, lead form, Page, Instagram account, and attribution setup after the client engagement is confirmed.
