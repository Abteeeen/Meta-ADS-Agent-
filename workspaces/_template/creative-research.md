# Creative Research

The raw material that feeds `creative-brief.md`. Collect angles, objections,
proof, competitor observations, and real customer language here first, so that
each ad concept can be traced back to something a customer actually feels or
says — not invented.

Relationship to other files:
- `customer-research.md` holds the priority segment and voice-of-customer
  evidence. Pull from it; do not duplicate it.
- `offer-and-positioning.md` holds the buyer promise and proof. Reference it.
- `creative-brief.md` turns the best items here into testable concepts.

Rules:
- Store exact customer language only when approved for storage and anonymized
  as needed. Attribute where each item came from.
- A claim used in an ad must be true and, where relevant, cleared for policy or
  legal review. Mark anything unverified.
- Do not present any item here as a Meta platform fact. These are inputs for
  creative, not performance guarantees.
