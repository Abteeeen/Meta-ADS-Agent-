# Reporting Note

A client-friendly weekly report. It is the outward-facing companion to
`weekly-review.md`: the internal review is where you do the thinking, and this
note is what the client reads. Keep it honest and plain — separate what
happened from what you think it means, and never dress up a guess as a fact.

Deliberately keeps five things apart: **facts**, **interpretation**,
**caveats**, **next actions**, and **what will not change yet**. This mirrors
the project's response contract (`docs/RESPONSE_CONTRACT.md`).
