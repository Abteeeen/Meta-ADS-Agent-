# Company Workspace Template

Start with the business context, not campaign settings. The agent should ask for missing inputs instead of filling them with generic best practices.

## Recommended sequence

1. `business-profile.md`: commercial reality, objective, unit economics, and constraints.
2. `offer-and-positioning.md`: what is being sold, why it matters, and why the buyer should believe it.
3. `customer-research.md`: jobs, pains, objections, language, and evidence.
4. `measurement-plan.md`: event, attribution context, source of truth, and acceptable economics.
5. `campaign-brief.md`: one proposed campaign and its assumptions.
6. `creative-brief.md`: testable creative concepts, not a pile of unconnected ads.
7. `decision-log.md` and `weekly-review.md`: retain the reasoning and results of every important change.
