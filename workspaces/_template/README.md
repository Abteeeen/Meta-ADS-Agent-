# Company Workspace Template

Start with the business context, not campaign settings. The agent should ask for missing inputs instead of filling them with generic best practices.

## Recommended sequence

1. `client-intake.md`: day-zero onboarding — offer, customer, economics, access, assets, and constraints.
2. `business-profile.md`: commercial reality, objective, unit economics, and constraints.
3. `offer-and-positioning.md`: what is being sold, why it matters, and why the buyer should believe it.
4. `customer-research.md`: jobs, pains, objections, language, and evidence.
5. `creative-research.md`: angles, objections, proof, competitor observations, and customer language that feed creative.
6. `measurement-plan.md`: event, attribution context, source of truth, and acceptable economics.
7. `campaign-brief.md`: one proposed campaign and its assumptions.
8. `creative-brief.md`: testable creative concepts, not a pile of unconnected ads.
9. `launch-qa.md`: pre-launch gate — nothing goes live until items pass with evidence or a blocker is accepted.
10. `decision-log.md` and `weekly-review.md`: retain the reasoning and results of every important change.
11. `reporting-note.md`: the client-facing weekly summary derived from `weekly-review.md`.

`first-30-days.md` sequences all of the above into a discovery → setup
validation → first campaign → learning loop plan for the first month.
